We want to find the dimensions of a rectangular prism with a volume of $250$ cubic inches of clay, and a height and width $5$ inches less than the length. To do this, 
say the length has a measurement of $x$, meaning the height and the width have both equal $x-5$. This means we have the following equations:
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
	& 250=(x-5)(x-5)x \\
	& 250=(x^2-10x+25)x \\
	& 250=x^3-10x^2+25x \\
	& x^3-10x^2+25x-250=0 \\
	& x^2(x)+x^2(-10)+25(x)+25(-10)=0 \\
	& (x^2+25)(x-10)=0 \\
	& x=\pm 5\vee x=10
\end{array}
\]
$-5$ cannot be accepted, as measurements must be positive. Also, $5$ cannot be accepted, because then the height would be $5-5=0$, which is not possible, as measurements must be 
positive and non-zero. Thus, $x=10$, meaning the dimensions of the prism are $10$ ft. long by $10-5=5$ ft. wide by $10-5=5$ ft. high.
