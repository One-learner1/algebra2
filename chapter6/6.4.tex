\section*{1}
A sample for which each member of a population has an equal chance of being selected is a random sample.

\section*{2}
An unbiased sample is a sample in which all groups of the population are represented. An unbiased sample is a sample in which certain portions of the population are either 
overrepresented or underrepresented. For example, an unbiased sample could occur if the sample had 90\% of its members from one group of the population, but that group 
is only 50\% of the population total population.

\section*{26}
We know that a sample size of $n$ is required to produce a margin of error of $\pm E$. Hence $\pm E=\pm\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}$.
Hence \[ \pm\frac{1}{2}E=\pm\frac{1}{2\sqrt{n}}=\pm\frac{1}{\sqrt{4n}} \] meaning the sample size must be increased by a factor of 4 to produce a margin of error of 
$\pm\frac{1}{2}E$.
