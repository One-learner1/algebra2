\section*{2}
Logarithmic equations may have extraneous solutions when one of the solutions is outside of the domain of the logarithmic function. For example, 
a negative solution for the equation $\log x=a$ would not be possible, as $\log x$ where $x\le 0$ is not defined.

\section*{61}
To find the time it takes the sunflower to reach a height of 200 centimeters, we can plug in $200$ for $h(t)$, and then solve for $t$:
\[ 200=\frac{256}{1+13e^{-0.65t}} \] \[ 200+2600e^{-0.65t}=256 \] \[ 2600e^{-0.65t}=56 \] \[ e^{-0.65t}=\frac{56}{2600} \]
\[ \ln (e^{-0.65t})=\ln \frac{56}{2600} \] \[ -0.65t\approx -3.84 \] \[ t\approx 5.9 \]
